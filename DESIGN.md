## Brand & Style

The design system projects craftsmanship, reliability, strength, and modern efficiency. Built for homeowners, architects, and trade professionals seeking premier renovation services and construction materials in San Fernando and the Bay of Cádiz, the UI balances functional directness with engaging, tactile visual storytelling. 

The aesthetic is Modern Tactile Corporate: a blend of high-utility digital architecture, clean structured layouts, expansive imagery showcasing raw materials and finished renovations, and decisive, highly touchable controls. It conveys solidity and professionalism through deep navy foundations, paired with vibrant construction-orange accents to guide key actions and highlight trade promotions.

## Colors

- **Primary (`#0D47A1`):** Deep marine corporate blue, establishing structural integrity, trust, and enterprise authority. Used for prominent headers, brand anchors, and high-priority primary actions.
- **Secondary (`#FF6B00`):** High-visibility safety orange. Applied selectively to draw immediate attention to promotional badges, primary quote conversion buttons, sale highlights, and energetic accents.
- **Tertiary (`#25D366`):** Dedicated WhatsApp conversion green, reserved exclusively for instant communication, direct phone links, and on-site support triggers.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC` to `#FFFFFF`):** Pure white cards set against an ultra-light slate foundation (`#F8FAFC`) with borders and dividers anchored in `#E2E8F0` and deep slate text (`#0F172A`).

## Typography

Typography pairs the structural, geometric presence of **Outfit** (in heavy weights `700`, `800`, and `900`) with the legibility and neutral balance of **Work Sans** for continuous reading and technical specifications.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Use `Outfit` Bold to Black. Headlines utilize tight tracking (`-0.02em`) to produce a solid, architectural presence.
- **Body & Specs:** Use `Work Sans` Regular (`400`) and Medium (`500`). Provides clear readability for material specs, quotes, project scopes, and regulatory notices.
- **Badges & Labels:** Use `Outfit` Bold or ExtraBold uppercase with slightly expanded letter-spacing (`+0.04em`) for punchy visual cues on tags and badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured on a 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (`min-width: 1024px`), an 8-column layout on tablet (`min-width: 640px`), and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Desktop:** Outer margin of `3rem` (`48px`) with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters. Content sections are capped at a maximum width of `1280px` to maintain optimal photographic and editorial impact.
- **Mobile/Tablet:** Gutters compress down to `1rem` (`16px`) with outer canvas margins of `1.25rem` (`20px`).
- **Rhythm & Photography:** Section padding leverages generous vertical whitespace (`space-xl` scaled up to `4rem` or `5rem` for major section dividers), allowing before-and-after photo galleries and material catalogs room to breathe without visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs ambient, diffused shadows combined with crisp, low-contrast structural borders:

- **Surface Neutral:** `#FFFFFF` resting on `#F8FAFC`.
- **Card Rest Elevation:** `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(13, 71, 161, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04); border: 1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Card Interactive Hover:** Transitions to `box-shadow: 0 12px 30px -4px rgba(13, 71, 161, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)` with a slight `-2px` vertical displacement.
- **Floating Modals & WhatsApp Quick Trigger:** `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design uses level `2` roundedness to provide modern, friendly ergonomics while retaining the structural rectangularity suited to construction:

- **Standard Base:** `0.5rem` (`8px`) for input fields, chips, small badges, and tooltips.
- **Cards & Visual Containers (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (`24px`) for project cards, image carousels, and quote form containers.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Interactive floating action buttons (such as the persistent WhatsApp floating contact button) and category filter pills utilize full pill geometry (`9999px`).

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary (Construction Action):* Solid `#0D47A1` background with white text, `rounded-lg` (12px), bold Outfit typography, large height (48px–56px) for thumb-friendly mobile tapping.
  - *Accent (Estimate/Quote):* Solid `#FF6B00` background, high contrast against white or dark navy sections.
  - *WhatsApp CTA:* Solid `#25D366` with high-contrast white text, paired with the WhatsApp icon. Used as a persistent corner floating element and in direct-quote card actions.
  - *Secondary / Ghost:* Transparent background with a 1.5px `#0D47A1` border and bold blue text.

- **Badges & Tags:**
  - Small, high-impact uppercase pills (`label-sm`).
  - *Promociones / Novedades:* `#FF6B00` background with pure white text or light tint (`#FFF7ED` fill with `#EA580C` text).
  - *In Stock / Disponibilidad Inmediata:* `#DCFCE7` fill with `#15803D` text.

- **Cards (Reformas & Materiales):**
  - Built with `rounded-xl` (24px) corners and fine `#E2E8F0` outlines.
  - Overflow-hidden media containers (16:9 or 4:3 aspect ratios) for construction works and material finishes.
  - Elevated metadata footers featuring clear specs (dimensions, coverage, installation time) and direct quote action triggers.

- **Before / After Comparison Sliders:**
  - Interactive split-image cards allowing horizontal drag comparisons of completed renovation projects in San Fernando.

- **Form Inputs & Quote Calculator:**
  - Inputs feature a robust 48px height, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, `#FFFFFF` surface, and focus ring in `#0D47A1` with an orange accent caret.
  - Checkboxes and radios use a bold 20px strike target with `#0D47A1` fill on active state.
